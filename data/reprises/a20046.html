Haiti - FLASH : Final results 2nd round elections 1/3 Senate
09/02/2017 04:51:58

The Provisional Electoral Council (CEP), in accordance with Article 171.1 of the Electoral Decree of 2 March 2015, published this Thursday the final results of the second round of the 1/3 Senate elections of January 29, 2017, for 5 departments for which there was no challenge.

Final results 2nd round elections 1/3 Senate - 29 January 2017

North West

Kedlaire Augustin (PHTK) 65.50% is elected

Francois Lucas Sainvil (MOSANOH) 32.28%

No candidates 2.23%

West

Patrice Dumont (Rassemblement des Patriotes Haïtiens - RPH) 57.40% is elected

Fednel Monchéry (PHTK) 40.13 %

No candidates 2.47%

Nippes

Denis Cadeau (Bouclier) 59.45% is elected

Louberson Vilson (Fanmi Lavalas) 39.44%

No candidates 1.11%

Grand Anse

Jean Rigaud Belizaire (Consortium) 52.83% is elected

Riche Andris (OPL) 45.92%

No candidates 1.25%

Artibonite

Garcia Delva (AAA) 53.85% is elected

Marc Antoine Aldorphe (Bouclier) 43.07%

No candidates 3.08%

The CEP informs that the preliminary results of the elections for the City Delegates of 29 January will also be available at the Electoral Operations Department, located at Route de Frères, in Petion-ville, at number 133, at the level of all Departmental Electoral Offices (BED) and Communal Electoral Offices (BEC). It should be noted that the Commune of Pestel did not have a candidate for the position of City Delegate.
