From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

This is a list of the 149 members of the 50th Parliament of Haiti. The House of Representatives is currently defunct as of 13 January 2020, as the terms of the representatives elected in the 2015-16 election expired.[1] 10 senators elected in 2016-17 retained their seat until 10 January 2023,[2] and after the expiration of those remaining senators' terms, Haiti has no elected members of either chamber of its Parliament.[3]
